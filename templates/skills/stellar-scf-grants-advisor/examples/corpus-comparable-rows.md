## How to pick comparables for Sheet 3

For each new dossier, pick 10 to 15 rows from this catalog plus the current dossier as row 1 (with "TBD" verdict). Pick a mix:

- 3 to 4 close-category winners (anchor and contrast).
- 4 to 5 close-category rejects (warning patterns triggered).
- 2 to 3 cross-category illustrations (bolt-on warning, SDF overlap warning, doxxing warning) if patterns apply.
- 2 in-flight targets (Rebond, For Yield, Solar Braves, Tickie) where the profile matches.

The Sheet 3 row order matters: closest comparable first after the current dossier. The client should be able to see at a glance "we look like X (winner) more than Y (reject)".
